01 / Our Philosophy & Craft

Elemental Texas Cooking, Guided by Mediterranean Restraint.

Ember & Olive was conceived around a simple culinary dialogue: the deep, resinous warmth of Central Texas post-oak fire paired with the bright, peppery clarity of early-harvest Hill Country olive oil. Rather than masking ingredients behind heavy sauces, we let live embers blister, caramelize, and concentrate what local growers bring to our back door each morning.

Our meats are sourced from family-run Texas pastures and dry-aged in-house for 35 days before meeting our custom iron grill grates suspended over white-hot oak coals.

Beside the hearth sits our glass-enclosed pasta table, where heritage durum semolina and Texas pasture-raised eggs are shaped into delicate agnolotti, saffron tagliolini, and ridged bronze-die rigatoni.

Whether you join us at the eight-seat hearth counter for a spontaneous glass of Sangiovese and charred flatbread, or gather around a walnut table for a full three-course supper, the pace is warm, attentive, and unhurried.

Designed for Unhurried Evenings

Warm limewash plaster, olive-hide banquettes, hand-turned walnut tables, and the steady crackle of post-oak embers create a room that feels both architectural and deeply welcoming.
